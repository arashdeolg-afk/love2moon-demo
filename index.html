What is Love2moon?

Love2moon is a couples companion app designed to help partners stay close and connected — whether they live together or are in a long-distance relationship. Every day, you and your partner answer a new question, check in on each other's mood, play games, and send virtual gifts.

The app is built around meaningful daily habits: thoughtful couples questions that spark real conversations, fun multiplayer games like Guess My Number and Would You Rather, a private chat for messages and reactions, and a virtual gift shop where you can send surprise treats that pop up on your partner's screen instantly.

With customizable Bitmoji-style avatars, streak tracking, and a growing library of couples games, Love2moon is the couples app for building closeness — one playful moment at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Is Love2moon free?
Yes — Love2moon is free to use. Create an account, pair with your partner using an invite code, and start playing right away.
Does Love2moon work for long-distance couples?
Absolutely. Love2moon is built with long-distance couples in mind. Daily check-in questions, real-time private chat, and virtual gifts are all designed so you can feel close no matter the distance.
What kind of questions does the app ask each day?
Each day you and your partner get a new couples prompt — things like "What song instantly reminds you of us?" or "When did you feel closest to me recently?" Once you both answer, you can see each other's replies and earn credits together.
What couple games are included?
Love2moon includes Guess My Number, Draw & Guess, Would You Rather, Truth or Dare, 36 Questions to Fall in Love, Two Truths and a Lie, Who Knows Who, Finish the Sentence, Guess My Answer, Story Builder, and more.
How do I pair with my partner?
Sign up, tap "Create a Couple" to get a unique invite code, and share it with your partner. They sign up and enter your code — you're instantly paired and ready to play.
